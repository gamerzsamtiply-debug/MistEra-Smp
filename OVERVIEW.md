# MistEra SMP — Official Website

The official site for MistEra SMP, a private, whitelist-only Minecraft Bedrock survival community based in Bangladesh (24/7 dedicated server).

**Audience:** players and visitors (public), registered community members, and the server's Owner/Admin team.

**What it does**
- Presents the server (IP/port, status, Discord, voting), a manageable "How to Join" whitelist guide, and the mandatory private-whitelist notice.
- Hosts announcements, updates, staff and partner directories, Information and Terms pages.
- Lets signed-in members keep a public profile, submit album screenshots (moderated) and suggestions.
- Gives the Owner (gamerzsamtiply@gmail.com) and Admins a protected panel to manage everything, with read-only activity and error logs and a maintenance mode.
